About me

Creative storyteller, multilingual communicator, and digital problem-solver.

I’m from Ghana, and my work lives at the intersection of language, health communication, and digital creativity. As a community health nurse with six years of professional experience, I understand the power of clear communication and culturally relevant storytelling.

I now bring that same impact into content creation, voice acting, transcription, translation, and script writing. I help brands become visible online while making sure their message connects naturally with Ghanaian audiences and beyond. I create AI-driven content, animations, and branded messages from stories, advertisements, and voiceovers to scripts and translations.

I am also expanding my technical skill set through software development, with a foundation in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Python. My goal is to combine creativity, language, and technology to build meaningful digital experiences.
